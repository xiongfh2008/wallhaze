# WallHaze licensed media sources

This file is the traceable source record for the eight motion demos and the
new tablet/desktop gallery wallpapers added in the 2026-10-24 quality pass.
All source photographs are **CC0** (public-domain dedication) from Wikimedia
Commons; the abstract "Soft Gradient" and "Light Ribbons" assets are original
procedural canvas art created for WallHaze.

Nothing in this pass is AI-generated. No paid image/video model was called.
No third-party asset without a recorded CC0/public-domain dedication was added.

## License audit method

- Audit date: **2026-10-24**.
- A second live audit queried Commons for all **15 source titles / 24 derived
  outputs**. Results are in `apps/web/scripts/media/license-audit.json`:
  all 24 outputs matched `LicenseShortName=CC0` and
  `AttributionRequired=false`, with 0 failures.
- `Copyrighted=True` in Commons extmetadata means its copyright-existence flag
  is true; the selected CC0 dedication is the operative distribution grant.
  This is not a claim that the source is un copyrighted.
- For every source file, the Commons `imageinfo.extmetadata` was read for
  `LicenseShortName`, `LicenseUrl`, `Artist` and `Credit`. CC0 was accepted only
  when the metadata reported **CC0** (not merely because a filename said so).
- The source page URL, original dimensions and license URL are recorded in
  `apps/web/scripts/media/photo-manifest.json`. Each output row records the
  original → derived crop relationship.
- Distribution scope: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — worldwide, irrevocable, commercial
  use permitted, attribution not required; credit is still shown in this record.
- Third-party rights: identifiable-person photos were **removed from the public
  set**. The production is now cherry blossoms, poppies, jellyfish, aurora,
  landscape, nebula and architecture without a prominent identifiable person.
  The Hong Kong street scene may contain incidental distant pedestrians/signage;
  no model or property release is claimed. The minimal interior crop removes the
  architecture-book covers; a small incidental frame edge may remain.
- This is a copyright/license audit, not a blanket clearance of personality,
  trademark or property rights in incidental background details.

## Licensing status in the product

- The original 39 gallery entries remain `review-pending`; no commercial
  license is asserted for them. Their 31 local style-preview files were moved
  from `apps/web/public/images/styles/` to
  `apps/web/private-media/review-pending/styles/` on 2026-10-24. Next.js does
  not serve that directory, so the old public URLs now return 404. No file was
  deleted; the reversible mapping and hashes are in
  `private-media/review-pending/manifest.json`.
- Six legacy styles and two retired hero/demo images were remote
  text-to-image URLs with no provider/model/rights evidence. They are stripped
  from the exported catalogue and no longer referenced. Their external URLs
  remain outside this site's control; see `docs/codex/MEDIA-ASSET-INVENTORY.md`.
- Full asset-level registry:
  `apps/web/data/media-rights.json` (82 assets: 43 verified, 31 pending,
  8 restricted). `node scripts/media/check-rights.mjs` verifies placement and
  hashes; `scripts/media/rights-admin.mjs` handles reviewed takedown/restore.
- Human-readable inventory: [MEDIA-ASSET-INVENTORY.md](./MEDIA-ASSET-INVENTORY.md).
- Takedown/review runbook: [MEDIA-RIGHTS-PROCESS.md](./MEDIA-RIGHTS-PROCESS.md).
- Final audit report: [COPYRIGHT-COMPLIANCE-REPORT.md](./COPYRIGHT-COMPLIANCE-REPORT.md).
- The 16 new photo wallpapers are marked `verified` only because every source
  is CC0 and traceable above. The detail page shows the source page, author and
  license.
- The two procedural additions are marked `self-authored`.
- User-generated media is separate: 30-day cloud retention and private signed
  URLs; it is never auto-published to Gallery.

## Themes intentionally not shipped

- **Magic floating islands / 魔法浮岛**: no licensed, high-quality source was
  found for a convincing result. A low-quality procedural placeholder was
  rejected; this theme needs an approved real AI video test.
- **Anime school-life / 四季风景 style previews**: no credible licensed
  anime/school or four-season preview was available. Existing `cozy-anime`,
  `anime-fantasy`, `nature` and `four-seasons`-adjacent styles remain
  available; no misleading photo was labelled as anime output.

The eight delivered demos cover sakura, neon rain, deep space, jellyfish,
aurora, sunset station, forest light and flower field with licensed photos and
local motion. This is deliberately a quality-first subset plus substitutions,
not a claim that all eight originally proposed themes were completed.
